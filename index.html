Pricing, roughly

Every home is different, so exact pricing comes from a free in-home walkthrough — but here's a general sense of how we price things.

  • Interior rooms are priced by square footage of wall/ceiling area, plus prep work needed (patching, priming) and number of coats.
  • Exterior jobs are priced by surface area, siding material, and how much scraping/prep the surfaces need.
  • Every quote is a flat price, in writing, before we start — no surprise charges. Materials are estimated up front and included in your price.
  • A deposit secures your spot on the schedule; the balance is due when the job's done and you're happy with it.
